Payment Policy. Monthly fees are charged on the first day of each month by UPI, card or auto debit. If a payment fails the system retries it after 3 days and again after 7 days. A late fee of INR 150 applies if a failed payment is not cleared within 10 days. If a member is charged twice for the same month, the extra amount is refunded within 7 working days. Refunds are never paid in cash.
