Membership Policy. Members may freeze a membership for a minimum of 15 days and a maximum of 60 days in a calendar year. A freeze request must be raised at least 3 days before the next billing date. A freeze fee of INR 200 is charged for each freeze. Premium members can freeze for free once per year. Memberships can be cancelled with 30 days written notice. Student plan members must show a valid student identity card at the front desk every 6 months.
